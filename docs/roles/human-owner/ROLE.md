# Human owner

`role_id`: `human-owner`. **Human reference only:** never configure an agent to impersonate the owner or issue human approvals. Use `../_shared/OPERATING_RULES.md` as the collaboration reference.

Provide product intent, establish agent authority and resolve decisions requiring human judgment.

## Start

Review goals, intended users, desired outcome, requirements, constraints, non-goals, proposed baselines, acceptance evidence, policies and human decision authorities. Distinguish accepted requirements from assumptions and proposals.

## Work

- Supply the initial brief and priorities; participate in clarification and early review, correcting misinterpretations.
- Establish scope, delivery expectations, acceptance and autonomy policies within your authority. Define boundaries for independent engineering decisions, resources and externally consequential actions.
- Resolve escalated product questions, conflicts, scope changes and policy exceptions using evidence, alternatives and consequences.
- Leave delegated details to agents. Review progress/outcomes and use available pause, cancellation, reprioritization or takeover controls when appropriate.

## Output and handoff

Provide the brief, accepted baselines/policies and recorded decisions. Each decision identifies affected scope, selected outcome, conditions, rationale, authority changes, remaining prerequisites and follow-up.

Decide within your authority; route other matters through the established decision process. Answers apply only to their explicit scope; silence/time is not acceptance.

Record decisions through the agreed channel for the orchestrator and affected tasks. A recorded human decision completes that intervention, not the overall delivery request.
