Setup rehearsal: this issue records a proposed delivery outcome. Labels and closure do not establish approval or acceptance.

## Project and origin

Identify the Project and link the originating Request or Requests. For an existing Initiative, explain the relationship to the new Request.

## Intended outcome and scope

Describe the overall result and its boundaries, including exclusions.

## Governing context

Link the applicable brief, specifications, Decisions, and constraints. Identify known revisions and distinguish accepted context from proposals or missing inputs.

## Overall acceptance expectations

Describe the observable outcome, required evidence, and designated acceptance authority when known. Link existing approval or delegation records; identify unresolved authority explicitly.

## Related Tasks

Link bounded contributions to this outcome. Tasks belong within the Initiative; keep execution prerequisites separate from scope membership.

## Questions and outstanding decisions

Record unresolved choices and their effect on delivery. Do not include credentials.
