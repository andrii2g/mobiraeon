Setup rehearsal: this is a Task candidate until validated. A kind label does not make it Ready, assign an Agent, or authorize execution.

## Initiative and origin

Link the containing Initiative and originating Request or Task where relevant. An origin link does not create a nested Task hierarchy.

## Objective, scope, and exclusions

Describe the bounded contribution and what is outside it.

## Required responsibility

Identify the needed responsibility, such as clarification, architecture, development, review, or verification. A Role name grants no permissions; do not choose runtime settings here.

## Inputs and governing context

Link required specifications, source revisions, Decisions, and other inputs. Identify their known revisions and whether they are accepted, proposed, or missing.

## Expected Deliverables

List the concrete results to submit, such as a document, patch, or verification report.

## Acceptance criteria and evidence

Describe observable checks and the evidence needed for these Deliverables. Distinguish this Task's acceptance from the Initiative's overall outcome.

## Prerequisites

Identify required accepted Deliverables or Decision resolutions and applicable revisions. Write "None" when there are no execution prerequisites; issue closure alone does not satisfy one.

## Authority and review expectations

Link the applicable baseline approval or delegation when known. Identify permitted scope, required independent review, and designated acceptance authority; record unknowns rather than assuming approval.

## Open questions

Record missing inputs, ambiguities, or blockers. Do not include credentials.
